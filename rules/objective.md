## 1. Objective & Brand Identity
- **Official Brand**: **Asesorías en Ingeniería** (superseding legacy 2024 *Euler Asesorías* flyers).
- **Mission**: Develop brand identity (primary logo, dark social profile badge, color system), high-converting Facebook ad images, animated MP4 videos, ad copy, and Messenger-to-WhatsApp sales scripts to attract high school, university, and professional students for private/online tutoring and engineering consulting—with an urgent focus on **end-of-semester final weeks**.

## 2. Core Tutoring Pillars
1. **Mathematics & Physics**: Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Calculus I/II/III, Differential Equations, Physics I/II/III.
2. **Civil & Structural Engineering**: Statics, Strength of Materials, Structural Analysis, NSR-10 Reinforced Concrete & Steel, Budgets/APUs.
3. **Programming & Engineering Software**: Python, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, MATLAB, Visual Basic, Bash, AutoCAD/FreeCAD.
4. **End-of-Term Intensive Prep**: Final exam workshops, last-week problem solving, and technical project mentoring.
